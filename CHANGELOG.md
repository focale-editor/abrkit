# 📰 AbrKit changelog

## v0.1.0
Released on August 24, 2026.

* **Initial release** with legacy ABR 1/2 and modern ABR 6/7/9/10 decoding.
* Decode sampled, computed, bristle, erodible, custom height-map, and airbrush tips.
* Decode Photoshop brush dynamics, textures, dual brushes, embedded patterns, tool options, and group hierarchy.
* Preserve complete Action Descriptors, unknown tagged sections, legacy payloads, bitmap metadata, and extension bytes.
* Support raw and PackBits bitmap data at 1, 8, and 16 bits, plus 32-bit pattern channels.
* Provide strict and tolerant modes with configurable resource limits.
